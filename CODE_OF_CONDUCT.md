# Code of conduct

Everyone is welcome to contribute, ask questions, and report problems, regardless of experience or background. This applies to this repository's issues, pull requests, reviews, and other project spaces, including interactions where someone represents the project.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and considerate. Help newcomers find the information they need.
- Give specific, constructive feedback about the work. Accept corrections and disagreement without personal attacks.
- Respect privacy and consent. Share only logs, screenshots, and personal information you have permission to share.
- Keep discussions relevant and follow maintainer guidance.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexual harassment, doxxing, sustained disruption, and personal insults are unacceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately using a contact method published on [the maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/hmason55). If no private contact method is listed, open an issue requesting a private contact channel without naming people or describing the incident publicly. Security vulnerabilities should follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Include links and context privately when a channel is available. Maintainers should protect the reporter's privacy, consider the circumstances, and respond proportionately. They may request edits, remove content, lock discussions, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Serious or repeated misconduct may lead to an immediate ban. Anyone handling a report about their own behavior should step aside where another maintainer is available.
